## Role-play

### Case Study: Crisis Communication at Tech Innovations Inc.

### Background

Tech Innovations Inc. experienced a crisis when a software update caused major system failures for several key clients, leading to downtime and complaints.

### Crisis Situation

- The update released on Friday resulted in significant disruptions, prompting immediate action.

- Customer service received a high volume of calls from concerned clients.

**Team Meeting:** Management held an emergency meeting on Monday with all department heads.

### Meeting Highlights

- CEO: Acknowledged the crisis and its potential impact on reputation.

- Product Development Lead: Briefed on technical issues and fix timeline.

- Customer Support Head: Discussed client concerns and emphasized empathetic responses.

- Sales Director: Suggested strategies to maintain client relationships.

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L17.pdf, page 2 -->

### Key Outcomes

- Commitment to open communication with clients, including updates and compensations.

- Formation of a crisis management task force.

### Analysis Questions

1. What signs indicated a crisis?

2. How effective was the team meeting communication?

3. Why is employee involvement important in communication at Tech Innovations Inc.?

4. What improvements could enhance their response plan?

### Discussion Questions

- What best practices should organizations adopt for effective crisis management?

- How can companies create a proactive crisis communication strategy?

- How can leaders promote open communication during crises?

- How should companies balance transparency with positive messaging in crises?

- What are the long-term effects of poor crisis communication on reputation, and how can this be addressed?

## Production

### ROLE-PLAY

1. Work in pairs

2. Each pair receives a crisis scenario (see options below).

3. Your task is to:

- Discuss what happened

- Plan how to respond

- Role-play a conversation with a manager, client, or colleague

4. You must use professional crisis communication phrases. After 5–10 minutes of preparation, groups act out their role-plays.

Crisis Scenarios

- A shipment of products arrived late and damaged.

- A team member accidentally sent a sensitive document to the wrong client.

- A system crash delayed all client orders for three hours.

- A public complaint about your company was posted on social media.

- The wrong invoice was sent to a client with incorrect pricing.

### Useful Phrases

1. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

2. We’re working on a solution and will keep you updated.

3. Let me explain what happened.

4. Here’s what we can do to fix the problem.

5. We understand your frustration and take this seriously.

6. The issue has been reported, and we are resolving it as fast as possible.

7. Can we offer a discount/credit/free service as an apology?

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L17.pdf, page 6 -->

Example Conversation

**Emma:** Hi Leo, the client just called. They received an invoice with the wrong amount.

**Leo:** Oh, I’m really sorry about that. I’ll correct it immediately and send the updated version.

**Emma:** Thanks. Please also apologize to the client in the email.

**Leo:** Of course. I’ll explain the mistake and offer to answer any questions.

**Emma:** Good. Please keep me informed once it’s sent.

**Leo:** Will do!

### Discussion

- What was the most difficult part of the situation to handle?

- How did your team show responsibility and professionalism?

- How could the situation be prevented in the future?
