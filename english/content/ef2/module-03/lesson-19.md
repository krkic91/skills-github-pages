## Warm-Up

## Case-study

### Study the cases below and answer the questions

**Sales-pitch 1:** SmartHome Automation System

Hello everyone, my name is Alex, and I’m excited to introduce you to our SmartHome Automation System. In today's fast-paced world, home automation is becoming essential for convenience and security. Our system allows you to control lights, thermostat, and security cameras from anywhere using your smartphone.

With features like voice control, energy monitoring, and customizable alerts, you can improve your home’s efficiency while ensuring your family's safety. Our customers have reported a significant decrease in their energy bills and an increased sense of security.

We would love to offer you a free consultation to tailor the system to your needs. Imagine coming home to a perfectly lit environment, all controlled by your voice or a simple tap on your phone. Thank you for your time, and I’m here to answer any questions you may have.

**Sales-pitch 2:** Generic Fitness Tracker

Hey, everyone. I want to talk about our new fitness tracker. It can count your steps and track your heart rate. I think you might find it helpful.

Some people say it’s good, and it has a few features. But honestly, it’s pretty similar to other fitness trackers out there. It’s not the cheapest option, but you can wear it on your wrist, which is cool.

You should probably buy it if you want to get fit, but I don’t have specific details or statistics right now. Just let me know if you have any questions.

### Role-play Analysis

1. What specific features of the SmartHome Automation System were highlighted, and how do they address customer needs?

2. How did the presenter use data or testimonials to support SmartHome Automation System?

3. How did the lack of specific details impact the audience's perception of the Generic Fitness Tracker?

4. How did the presenter’s tone and confidence affect the credibility of the Generic Fitness Tracker pitch?

5. What suggestions would improve the presentation of the Generic Fitness Tracker to make it more persuasive?

### Discussion

- What are the essential steps or elements that should be included in an effective sales pitch?

- If you were the customer, what other questions would you ask before making a decision?

- What is the best way to handle questions or objections during a sales pitch?

## Sales-pitch elements

| Part of Sales<br>Pitch | Brief Definition | Key Phrases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | The introduction that sets<br>the tone and engages the<br>audience. |  "Thank you for being here today."<br> "I appreciate the opportunity to<br>present." |
| Establishing<br>Need | Identifies the problem or<br>need that the product<br>addresses. |  "Many people face challenges with..."<br> "This need has been growing<br>because..." |
| Product<br>Features | Describes the<br>characteristics and<br>functionalities of the<br>product or service. |  "Our product offers several key<br>features..."<br> "This feature allows you to..." |
| Supporting<br>Evidence | Provides data,<br>testimonials, or case<br>studies to support claims. |  "According to research conducted<br>by..."<br> "We have received positive feedback<br>from clients such as..." |
| Call to Action | Encourages the audience<br>to take a specific action. |  "I invite you to try [Product/Service]..."<br> "Take the next step and join us." |
| Closing | Summarizes key points<br>and expresses gratitude. |  "In summary, [Product/Service] can<br>help you..."<br> "Thank you for your time." |

## Production

### QUICK SALES PITCH

1. Students go into pairs.

2. Choose a product (real or imaginary)

3. Prepare the Sales Pitch: Using the structure in the lesson, each pair will outline their pitch, including:

- Opening: Introduce yourself and the product.

- Need: Explain the problem the product addresses.

- Features: Highlight key features of the product.

- Supporting Evidence: Provide any relevant statistics or testimonials.

- Call to Action: Encourage action (e.g., “Try it today!”).

- Closing: Summarize key points and thank the audience.

4. Present to the Class

- Encourage enthusiastic delivery and the use of persuasive language.

5. Receive Peer Feedback
