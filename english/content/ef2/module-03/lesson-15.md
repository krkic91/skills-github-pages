## Production

### Instructions: Work in teams or pairs. Present or role-play the scenario based on the

given objective.

**Case Study 1:** Virtual Presentation

- Objective: Effectively present a product and address investor questions.

- Scenario: You are a marketing team presenting a new product launch to potential investors via video conference.

**Case Study 2:** Negotiation

- Objective: Reach a mutually beneficial agreement on the contract terms.

- Scenario: You are a sales team negotiating a contract with a potential client.

**Case Study 3:** Delegating Tasks

- Objective: Effectively delegate tasks and ensure team understanding.

- Scenario: You are a project manager assigning tasks to team members for an upcoming deadline.

**Case Study 4:** Problem-solving in Teams

- Objective: Collaborate to identify solutions and implement plan.

- Scenario: Your team is facing a productive delay due to equipment failure.
