## Warm-Up

### Motivation Questions

- Why is it important to write clear and polite requests in a business setting?

- Can you think of a situation where a well-written apology made a difference in a business relationship?

- How do you ensure your requests and apologies are understood and accepted by the recipient?

## Sample Request and Apology Emails

Study the following sample emails

**Subject:** Request for Information

Dear John,

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to request information regarding the upcoming project deadlines. Could you please provide the updated timeline at your earliest convenience?

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,

Anne Smith

**Subject:** Apology for Missed Deadline

Dear Anne,

I am writing to sincerely apologize for missing the project deadline. Due to unforeseen circumstances, we were unable to complete the task on time. We understand the inconvenience this has caused and are taking steps to ensure it does not happen again.

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,

John Dee

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L03.pdf, page 3 -->

### Analysis Questions

- What elements make these emails clear and effective?

- How does the structure help the recipient understand the purpose of the email?

- What could be added to ensure all necessary information is included?

### Discussion

- What are some common mistakes to avoid when writing requests and apologies?

- How can you make sure your request is polite yet assertive?

- What are some strategies to ensure your apology is sincere and accepted?

## Common Phrases

### KEY PHRASES FOR WRITING REQUESTS

| Contents | Phrases |
| --- | --- |
| Opening Statements |  “I hope this email finds you well.”<br> “I am writing to request your assistance with...”<br> “I would like to ask for your help regarding...” |
| Making the Request |  “Could you please…?”<br> “Would it be possible for you to...?”<br> “I would appreciate it if you could...” |
| Providing Context |  “This request is important because...”<br> “Due to the upcoming deadline...”<br> “I would like to ensure that... by [specific date].” |
| Closing Statements |  “Thank you for considering my request.”<br> “Please let me know if you need any further<br>information.”<br> “I look forward to your response.” |

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L03.pdf, page 4 -->

### KEY PHRASES FOR WRITING APOLOGY EMAILS

| Contents | Phrases |
| --- | --- |
| Opening Statements |  “I hope this email finds you well.”<br> “I am writing to sincerely apologize for...” |
| Acknowledging the Issue |  “I apologize for any inconvenience this may have<br>caused.”<br> “I acknowledge that this situation may have<br>affected you.” |
| Taking Responsibility |  “Full responsibility for the situation has been<br>acknowledged.”<br> “I am committed to making things right and<br>learning from this experience.” |
| Offering an Explanation (if<br>appropriate) |  “The reason for this was...”<br> “Unfortunately, this occurred due to...” |
| Proposing a Solution |  “To rectify this situation, I will...”<br> “I am taking steps to ensure this does not happen<br>again by...”<br> “I would like to offer [compensation or solution].” |
| Closing Statements |  “Thank you for your understanding and patience.”<br> “Please let me know if there’s anything else I can<br>do to resolve this matter.” |

### 4. Feedback Review: Review your partner's request and provide feedback on

- Clarity: Is the request easy to understand?

- Specificity: Are the details of the request clear and specific?
