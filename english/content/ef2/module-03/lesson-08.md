## Role-play

### Scenario: A team meeting involving members from two different cultures: a German

participant (Anna) and a Japanese participant (Kenji) discussing a project timeline.

### Dialogue

**Anna:** Good morning, everyone. I’d like to review the project timeline. I think we need to set clear deadlines to ensure we stay on track.

**Kenji:** Good morning. I agree that deadlines are important, but I believe we should also consider the need for flexibility in our schedule, especially when dealing with unexpected challenges.

**Anna:** Flexibility is good, but I think if we don't set firm deadlines now, we might end up missing our final deadlines later.

**Kenji:** In Japan, we often prefer to create harmony and ensure that everyone is on board. Maybe we can have a checkpoint meeting later in the timeline to reassess our progress.

**Anna:** That sounds reasonable. But I want to ensure that we all take responsibility for our parts. What do you think about setting milestone deadlines?

**Kenji:** I see your point. It might be helpful to have both firm milestones and a flexible approach. How about we discuss this with the entire team and reach a consensus?

### Role-Play Analysis

1. What cultural differences did you notice in the dialogue between Anna and Kenji?

2. How did each participant express their ideas about deadlines?

3. What strategies did Kenji use to address Anna's concerns while maintaining harmony?

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L08.pdf, page 3 -->

### Discussion

- What are some common communication barriers in cross-cultural meetings?

- How can you ensure clear communication when working with colleagues from different cultural backgrounds?

- What strategies can be used to improve cross-cultural understanding and collaboration?

### ACTIVITY 2: CROSS-CULTURAL ROLE-PLAY AND DISCUSSION

### Instructions

- Students go into groups, each group has members representing a different cultural perspective (Indian, Japanese, American, Vietnamese).

- Each group divides roles to member to role-play a negotiation situation involving a project disagreement within a multicultural team.

### Negotiation Situation

The team needs to finalize the budget for a new project, but each culture has different perspectives on how to allocate resources.

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L08.pdf, page 6 -->

### Roles

- Indian Members: Focus on building relationships and seeking agreement.

- Japanese Members: Prioritize group consensus and indirect communication.

- American Members: Push for quick resolution and direct decisions.

- Vietnamese Leader: Mediate and encourage discussion.

### Role-Play Instructions

- Groups will be given 10 minutes to prepare their positions and strategies based on their cultural values.

- Each group will then participate in a structured 15-minute negotiation session where they must discuss and debate budget allocation while adhering to their cultural perspectives.

- The Vietnamese leader will facilitate the discussion and ensure everyone participates

### Debrief

1. What challenges did you encounter, and how did you overcome them?

2. How did the leader facilitate or hinder effective communication?

3. What strategies could be applied in actual cross-cultural situations to improve outcomes?
