## Case-study

### PROJECT SETBACK IN SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

**Background:** Tech Solutions Inc. is a software development company working on a new application intended to streamline project management for small businesses. The project started positively, but two months in, the team begins to face significant challenges that threaten the launch timeline.

**Current Situation:** The development team has encountered multiple technical issues, including integration problems with third-party software and unexpected delays in feature implementation. Additionally, team morale has decreased as members are frustrated with the ongoing issues and experience communication breakdowns during

<!-- Source: F2_M03_L20.pdf, page 2 -->

team meetings. The project manager, Laura, must address these challenges and bring the project back on track.

Case-study Analysis

1. What are the main challenges impacting the project?

2. How can Laura improve communication among team members?

3. What steps can be taken to address the integration issues?

4. What strategies can be implemented to enhance team motivation and productivity?

5. What measures can be adopted to prevent similar setbacks in future projects?

## Production

### Instructions

- Use the target phrases below to create your own short dialogues or sentences.

- You can work in pairs (for speaking) or individually (for writing).

- Choose at least 5 phrases and use them in a realistic business situation (e.g. writing an email, solving a problem, or talking in a meeting).

1. Could you please send me the report by...

2. I’m writing to apologize for...

3. Let’s schedule a meeting to discuss...

4. The purpose of this email is to...

5. Please let me know if you need any help.

6. Here is a quick update on the project...

7. We need to find a solution to...

8. Thanks for your understanding.

9. I’d like to delegate this task to...

10. I suggest we meet online next week.
