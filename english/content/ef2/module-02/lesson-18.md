## Role-Play

### Scenario: David, a project manager, needs to give a project update to his team leader,

John.

**David:** Hi, John. I wanted to give you a quick update on the project.

**John:** Sounds good. What’s the latest?

**David:** Here’s the latest update, we’re currently on track to meet the deadline. However, we encountered some issues with the design phase which initially made me worried that we might be behind schedule with the project’s deliverables.

**John:** What kind of issues?

**David:** We had some miscommunication with the design team, this bottleneck threatened to cause delays in meeting the project’s milestone. But we’ve worked it out, and everything is back on track.

**John:** Okay, good. What’s the plan for the next phase?

**David:** We’re going to start testing next week. I’ll send you a detailed plan by the end of the day.

### Role-Play Analysis

Answer the following questions:

1. According to David, what was the cause of the design phase delays?

2. What can you do to avoid miscommunication with the team?

3. How did John confirm the team’s next steps?

4. What is the team’s next plan?

5. What do you think will be the impact of delays on the project?

2. A bottleneck in a project is a point where progress is accelerated. (True/False)

3. Deliverables are the intermediate steps in a project. (True/False)

4. A milestone is a significant event or achievement in a project. (True/False)

5. A project is behind schedule when it is ahead of its planned timeline. (True/False)

## Production

### MINI ROLE-PLAY: GIVING UPDATES

### Instructions: Practice providing a project update in a clear and concise manner in the

following scenarios:

**Scenario 1:** You’re managing a software development project, and you’ve just completed the coding phase. Explain the project update to your team, including any next steps for the testing phase.

**Scenario 2:** Your project is facing budget constraints, and you need to adjust the scope to stay within budget. Explain the project update to your team, highlighting the changes that will be made.

**Scenario 3:** You’ve completed the first phase of your research project, and the results are promising. Explain the project update to your research partner, discussing the implications of the results and the next steps.

### Discussion

1. What specific language or phrases did you use to communicate important information effectively?

2. Did you encounter any challenges in conveying updates in your scenario? How did you overcome them?

3. How important is it to keep your team or partner informed about project progress?

4. How did you feel about delivering project updates, and what would you improve for future updates?

## Key Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On Track | Used in progressing as<br>planned or in meeting<br>goals and objectives | The project is currently on<br>track to meet its deadline of<br>April 15th. |
| Behind schedule | Used in a situation where<br>a project, task, or activity is<br>not progressing as<br>planned and is falling<br>behind the expected<br>deadline. | Unfortunately, the project is<br>currently behind schedule<br>due to unforeseen delays in<br>the development phase. |
| Milestone | Used in a significant event<br>or achievement that marks<br>a major point in a project. | We’ve reached a major<br>milestone in the project,<br>completing the design phase<br>ahead of schedule. |
| Deliverables | Used in referring to the<br>specific products or<br>services that are expected<br>to be delivered as part of a<br>project. | The project team has<br>completed the following<br>deliverables: design<br>documents and user<br>feedback analysis. |
| Bottleneck | Used when the flow of<br>work, resources, or<br>information is restricted or<br>slowed down, causing<br>delays. | We’ve identified a<br>bottleneck in the testing<br>phase, which is causing<br>delays in the project timeline. |
| Here’s the latest<br>update… | This expression clearly<br>signals that new<br>information will be<br>provided and used to<br>provide a brief and on<br>point update. | Here’s the latest update on<br>my task list and the<br>challenges I’m facing. |
