## Role-Play

**Scenario 1:** Two team members, Anna and Michael, must decide who will present at the next client meeting.

**Anna:** Hi Michael, I see both our names are down to present at the client meeting. I was looking forward to doing it, but I know you were too.

**Michael:** That’s right. I’d like to present as well. What would be a fair solution? Maybe we can find a middle ground?

**Anna:** Good idea. How about we both give a little? We could split the presentation so each of us has a part.

**Michael:** I like that suggestion. I’m willing to adjust if you are, too.

**Anna:** Yes, let’s meet halfway and share the presentation.

**Michael:** Great, let’s discuss our parts and make sure it’s smooth.

<!-- Source: F2_M02_L14.pdf, page 2 -->

**Scenario 2:** Two team leaders, Maria and Henry, are deciding how to allocate a limited budget between their projects.

**Maria:** Henry, the budget is very tight this quarter, but both our projects are important. Let’s work together to find a solution.

**Henry:** I was thinking the same. How can we both get what we want?

**Maria:** Maybe we can combine our ideas to make it work for both of us. If we adjust our timelines, maybe each project can get what it needs most right now.

**Henry:** That makes sense. What if we try to satisfy both sides and look for ways to share resources too?

**Maria:** Good idea. Let’s brainstorm options together and create a proposal for management.

**Henry:** Perfect. I’m confident we can find a way that fits both teams.

### Role-play Analysis

1. What are the main problems in each dialogue?

2. How do the speakers show respect for each other’s ideas and needs in these dialogues?

| Strategies | Definitions |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Avoiding | Ignoring or postponing the conflict instead of addressing it. |
| 2. Accommodating | Putting others’ needs before your own and giving in to their<br>preferences. |
| 3. Competing | Trying to win the conflict with your own needs, with little<br>regard for others. |
| 4. Compromising | Both sides give up something to reach a mutual agreement. |
| 5. Collaborating | Working together to find a solution that fully satisfies<br>everyone’s needs. |
