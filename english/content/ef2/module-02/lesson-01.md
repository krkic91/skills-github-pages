## Role-Play

### Scenario: Project Manager, Alden, is having a kick-off meeting for the new project with

his two team members, Kath and Daniel.

**Alden:** All right, let’s get started. Good morning, everyone. Welcome to the kick-off meeting for our new project.

**Kath:** Good morning, Alden.

**Alden:** The aim of this meeting is to review the project objectives, timeline, and roles and responsibilities. We’ll also discuss our communication plan and expectations.

**Daniel:** Sounds like we’ve got a lot to cover.

**Alden:** Yes, we do. But first, let’s go over the review of the project objectives. (shares presentation, followed by timeline and roles and responsibilities.)

**Kath and Daniel:** Looks good and clear.

**Alde:** Great. Now, let’s discuss our communication plan. We’ll be having regular team meetings, and I encourage everyone to speak up and share their thoughts and ideas.

**Kath:** Sounds good.

### Alden: Alright, that’s all for today. Let’s get started on this project!

## Useful Phrases in Opening a Meeting

1. The aim/purpose of today’s meeting is… – used to clearly state the meeting’s purpose and establish expectations for the meeting outcomes.

### Example

- The aim/purpose of this meeting is to discuss the current project status, address any challenges, and outline the next steps.

2. First, let’s go over… / Before we begin… – used in reviewing the agenda, discussing objectives, or reviewing previous meeting.

### Example

- First, let’s go over the agenda for today's meeting to ensure we cover all the necessary topics.

- Before we begin, I’d like to review the agenda for today's meeting to ensure we cover all necessary topics.

3. Let’s get started – used to signal the beginning of the meeting and encourage attendees to focus and engage.

### Example

- Now that we have everyone here, let’s get started. Our first topic is…

## Practice 2

### Instructions

Team up with a partner and start a typical meeting. Capture everyone’s attention and apply effective words and phrases to set the tone for the discussion. Make sure to engage participants and establish a welcoming atmosphere for discussion.

### Tips

By having clear purpose and objectives, concise agenda review, establishing expectations, and encouraging engagement into your meeting opening, you can set the stage for a productive, efficient, and effective meeting.

## Production

Be more confident in opening a meeting by answering the following questions.

1. How can you ensure that all attendees are aware of the meeting’s purpose or agenda?

2. How can you ensure that all attendees are engaged and focus from the beginning?

3. How can you ensure that all attendees understand their roles and responsibilities during the meeting?

4. How can you create a safe and respectful environment for open discussion?

5. How can you keep a meeting on track and ensure that all topics were covered?
