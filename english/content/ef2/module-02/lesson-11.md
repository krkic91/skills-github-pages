## Role-Play

### Scenario: Two colleagues are talking about their recent job promotions. Read and study

the words in bold.

**Mitch:** Hey, Johnny! How did your negotiation with HR go regarding your job promotion?

**Johnny:** Oh, it was quite a process. I had to bargain a lot to get them to see my point of view.

**Mitch:** Really? What did you propose?

**Johnny:** I proposed a new role that would align better with my skills and experience.

**Mitch:** That sounds intense. Did you have to compromise on anything?

**Johnny:** Yes, I had to compromise on the salary a bit. But in the end, we struck a deal that included better benefits.

**Mitch:** That's great! It sounds like you managed to get a fair deal. I had a similar experience last year. It took a while to find common ground, but once we did, the compromise was worth it.

**Johnny:** Absolutely. It's all about finding that balance and making sure both sides feel like they've won something.

## Key Phrases

### EFFECTIVE OPENING STATEMENTS

1.When you negotiate to a proposal – you can express your agreement by saying

- [x] Would you be able to + [ V ] - your proposal

- [x] Would you consider + [ V + ing ] your proposal

### Example

- [x] Would you be able to review my annual evaluation with a 10% increase?

- [x] Would you consider evaluating my job performance for a 10% increase?

2.When you negotiate to a proposal – you can express your disagreement by saying

- [x] That’s not quite what I had in mind + your proposal

- [x] Let’s find a fair solution + your proposal

### Example

- [x] That’s not quite what I had in mind, and I feel like you can make an increase from 5% to 10%.

- [x] Let’s find a fair solution and compromise on 10% as the best number.

## Production

### MINI CASE STUDY

### Instructions

- Work in pairs. One student will be Person A, and the other will be Person B.

- Read your role carefully and think about your needs and priorities.

- Take turns speaking. Use polite and clear negotiation phrases you have learned (e.g., "Can we find a middle ground?", "What if...", "I’m afraid that won’t work…", "That works for me.").

- Listen actively to your partner. Show understanding even if you don’t agree.

- Try to reach a fair solution that works for both people.

- When you finish, switch roles and try again with new ideas or different phrasing.

### SCENARIOS

**Scenario 1:** Shift Swap

**A:** You want to take Friday off for a family event.

**B:** You have a doctor’s appointment that day. → Discuss and try to find a solution.

### Example ideas to include

“Can we find a middle ground?”

“What if I cover your shift next Monday instead?”

<!-- Source: F2_M02_L11.pdf, page 6 -->

**Scenario 2:** Team Task

**A:** You’re asked to lead a project, but you already have too much work.

**B:** You think A should do it because they have more experience. → Try to agree on who should lead, or how to divide the task.

### Helpful phrases

“I see your point, but…”

“We could split the work this way…”

**Scenario 3:** Working from Home

**A:** You want to work from home two days a week.

**B:** Your manager is not sure it’s a good idea. → Talk about the pros and cons and suggest a compromise.

### Useful ideas

“Let’s look at the options.”

“How about we try it for two weeks?”

**Scenario 4:** Rescheduling a Meeting

**A:** You’re asked to join a meeting at 5:30 p.m., but you can’t.

**B:** You think it’s important and don’t want to change the time. → Try to agree on a new time.

### Useful ideas

“Let’s look at the options:..”

<!-- Source: F2_M02_L11.pdf, page 7 -->

**Scenario 5:** Training Opportunity

**A:** You are invited to attend a training abroad, but you have deadlines.

**B:** You want to go too, but can’t both go. → Negotiate who should attend and suggest solutions.

### Useful ideas

“We could consider...”

“How about we flip a coin, or ask the manager for advice?”

“That works for me if…”

### Tips for Speaking

- Use at least 3 of the phrases from the lesson.

- Speak naturally and be polite.

- Don’t forget to make suggestions and try to compromise.
