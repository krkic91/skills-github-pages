## Role-Play

### Scenario: John, a project manager, needs to give a project update to his team member,

Alex, on the project’s progress.

**Version 1:** Unclear Update

**John:** Hey Alex, I just wanted to let you know that we’re working on some stuff, and it’s going okay, I guess. We’ll get it done eventually.

**Alex:** (looks confused) What do you mean by ‘some stuff’? To keep it brief, which tasks are we talking about? And what’s the deadline? Could you please summarize the key points for all the tasks?

**Version 2:** Concise Update

**John:** Hey Alex, just a quick update to outline the progress of the project’s tasks. We’ve completed tasks A and B and we’re on track to finish task C by Friday. However, we’re experiencing a delay with task D. The key issue is miscommunication with the design team, which might impact our overall deadline.

**Alex:** (nods) Got it, thanks for the update. What’s the plan to address the delay with task

D?

### Role-Play Analysis

Answer the following questions:

1. How did Alex respond differently to the two updates?

2. What are the benefits of providing concise updates?

3. How can you apply this to your own project updates?

4. What are common pitfalls to avoid when providing updates?

### Scenario: James is managing a software development project, and he is giving an

update to the project owner, Henry.

**Henry:** Can you give me an update on the project?

**James:** To __________ the current project status, I’d like to highlight the following __________: we’ve completed the coding phase and are moving forward with testing.

**Henry:** That’s great to hear. What’s the biggest challenge you’re facing right now?

**James:** __________ that we’re experiencing some technical difficulties with the new API integration, which might impact our timeline.

**Henry:** Okay, I understand. Can you give me a quick overview of the new project’s progress?

**James:** To __________, we’ve made significant advancements in the past quarter and are on track to meet our deadline. __________, I’ll focus on the main points and avoid unnecessary details.

- Initial set-up has been completed.

### Context

- The project is on track.

- The main issue is the limited budget.

- A progress report will be prepared once the budget discussion with the finance team is finalized.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word/Phrase | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summarize | Used to provide a brief and<br>concise overview of the<br>main points, ideas, or key<br>information of a larger<br>work or situation. | To summarize, we’ve made<br>significant progress on the<br>project, completing phase 1<br>and 2, and on track to meet<br>the deadline. |
