## Role-Play

At a networking event.

**James:** Hi My name is James. What is your name?

**Kath:** Hello, James! I'm Kath

**James:** Nice to meet you, Kath. How are you?

**Kath:** I'm good, thank you. And you?

**James:** I'm good too! Are you here for the event?

**Kath:** Yes, I am. I'm learning about marketing. How about you?

**James:** I'm new to business, but I want to learn more.

**Kath:** That's great! Marketing is interesting.

**James:** Yes, it sounds exciting. How long have you been learning marketing?

**Kath:** I've been learning for a year. It's fun!

**James:** Can I have your email to stay in touch?

**Kath:** Sure! It's kath.marketing@gmail.com

**James:** Thank you, Kath! I'll email you soon.

**Kath:** You're welcome, James. I look forward to hearing from you.

**James:** Bye, Kath! Have a nice day!

**Kath:** Bye, James! Take care!

### Role-Play Analysis

Answer the following questions:

1. How did the conversation start?

2. How did the participants maintain a polite and professional tone?

3. How did James express interest in continuing the relationship?

## Production

Networking at a Conference

### Instructions

1. You are attending a Business Conference related to your field.

2. Network with other attendees, exchange information, and make valuable connections.

3. Networking skills to be highlighted:

* Starting conversations * Asking open-ended questions * Active listening * Sharing information about themselves * Closing conversations politely

**Roles:** Job seeker, Entrepreneurs, Company Representative, Industry Experts
