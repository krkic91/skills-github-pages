## Role-Play

### Scenario: A new Memo from HR suggests that more alternative forms of work modes

will be introduced in the office. HR is seeking employees’ suggestions and recommendations. Study the conversation between two employees.

**Thuy:** How are things in your department Brian? Have you thought about the recent memo from HR?

**Brian:** Uhm, I guess, this news has been the talk of the office for some time now. So, have you weighed the pros and cons of hybrid work?

**Thuy:** To be honest, I’d suggest that working from home on some days and working on-site is a winning choice for us.

**Brian:** For me, I’d consider either only working from home or just working at the office, never hybrid.

**Thuy:** Why don’t you recommend that to your supervisor and email them about what you think?

**Brian:** That seems like a good idea, and sure I will propose that!

**Thuy:** I’ll finalize my thoughts after this week.

**Brian:** Let’s reach an agreement after this week!

## Production

**A:** Solving a Workplace Problem

### Workplace Problems (choose one)

1. Several employees are always late for meetings.

2. Two departments are not communicating well.

3. The office is too noisy for focused work.

4. Many staff are stressed because of too much overtime.

5. Employees are using phones too much during working hours.

### Instructions

- Choose one problem together.

- Student A: Make one suggestion to solve it.

<!-- Source: F2_M01_L13.pdf, page 4 -->

- Student B: Respond, add another suggestion.

- Discuss and agree on one final solution.

- (Optional) Share your decision with the class.

**B:** Planning a Company Event

### Requirements

- You and your partner are planning a company event (for example: a team-building day, an anniversary party, a training workshop).

- Make suggestions about the event and decide together on the best plan.

### You must decide together

- What kind of event will it be?

- Where will it take place?

- What activities will you include?

- What rules or guidelines will you set?

### Instructions

1. Student A suggests the type of event and the place.

2. Student B responds: agree or suggest something different.

3. Together, make at least three decisions about the event.

4. Prepare to share your final plan with the class.
