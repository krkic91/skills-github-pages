## Warm-up

Think about the last time you met a new colleague or business contact.

How did you introduce yourself?

If you could improve your introduction, what would you change?

## Role-Play

Practice Introducing Yourself (10 minutes)

**Example Format:** [Self-Introduction Structure]

**Greeting:** “Hello, let me introduce myself.”

**Name and Role:** “I’m [Name], and I work in [Industry/Role].”

**Experience and Interests:** “I have been in this field for [X years] and specialize in [Skill/Area].”

**Engagement:** It’s great to meet professionals like you!”

### Role-Play Analysis

1. Was your introduction clear and well-structured?

2. Did you include key details (name, role, company, and experience?

3. Did you sound confident and natural, or did you hesitate?

4. How could you improve your tone or body language even in a virtual setting)?

5. Did you make the conversation engaging by asking a follow-up question?

## Production

Speed networking challenge

1. Pair up with a partner.

2. Introduce yourself using formal greetings and key networking phrases (e.g., “Let me introduce myself. [work] as [role] at [company].”

3. Ask & respond to networking questions (e.g., ‘What brings you here today?” or “Would you like to exchange LinkedIn profiles?”)

4. Engage in conversation for 2-3 minutes, then switch partners.

5. Sound natural and confident

6. Wrap up by sharing one takeaway from the conversation.
