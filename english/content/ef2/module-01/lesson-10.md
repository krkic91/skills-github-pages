2. Debate: What’s the Best Way to Communicate?

### Work in small groups and join in a debate

**Debate Topic:** “In business, being direct is better than being polite.”

- When is direct communication helpful or necessary?

- Can polite language sometimes cause confusion?

- What do different cultures expect in feedback?

- Is it possible to be both clear and respectful?

### Instructions

- Choose your side: Agree or Disagree.

- In your group, prepare 2–3 arguments to support your opinion.

- Introduce your group’s opinion and take turns sharing your ideas.

### Useful phrases

- To give your opinion: I believe that…; In my experience…; I think email is better because…

- To disagree politely: I see your point, but…; I’m not sure I agree with that;

Another way to look at it is…;

- To support your point: For example…; One reason is…; This is especially true when…

## Production

## ROLE-PLAY: TECHNICAL SUPPORT ACROSS CULTURE

### Instructions

1. Form pairs – one student plays Tech Support (A), the other plays Client (B).

2. Choose a scenario (or rotate through all three).

3. Read the scenario brief and understand the technical issue and cultural element involved.

4. Role-play the support call using appropriate language and tone. Switch roles after completing one scenario.

<!-- Source: F2_M01_L10_v0.2.pdf, page 4 -->

**Scenario 1:** Software Installation Glitch

**Technical Problem:** The client is receiving error messages while trying to install new software.

**Cultural Element:** The client comes from a culture where direct questions are seen as impolite.

**Support Focus:** Be patient and use indirect or polite questioning to gather details.

Provide clear, step-by-step instructions.

**Scenario 2:** Network Connectivity Issues

**Technical Problem:** The client is experiencing intermittent internet connection, affecting their ability to work.

**Cultural Element:** The client is from a culture with high levels of formality.

**Support Focus:** Use formal language and maintain a professional, respectful tone while troubleshooting.

**Scenario 3:** Delayed System Update

**Technical Problem:** The client’s system is running slowly due to a missed system update.

**Cultural Element:** The client values efficiency and time very highly and is becoming frustrated.

**Support Focus:** Stay calm and empathetic. Be concise, solution-focused, and reassuring.

### Discussion

- What challenges did you encounter when trying to balance technical support with cultural sensitivity?

- How does this activity relate to real-life situations where you've interacted with someone from a different cultural background?
