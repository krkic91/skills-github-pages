## Warm-up

### Discussion

What departments does your company have? Who do you report to in your company? What is their role? Do you think a clear company structure is important? Why or why not?

## Role-play

### Scenario: Company Structure Introduction

### Role-Play Dialogue

**John:** Welcome to Global Solutions, James! Let me give you a quick overview of our company structure. Our company is structured into multiple departments. You’ll be joining the

Software Development Department. This team is responsible for developing and optimizing our backend systems.

**James:** Got it. Who will I be reporting to?

**John:** Your direct report is Sarah Nguyen, the Team Lead for Backend Development.

**James:** And who does she report to?

**John:** Sarah reports to Daniel Lee, the Engineering Manager. He’s your line manager.

**James:** I see. How does communication flow within the company?

**John:** We follow a clear company hierarchy with a chain of command. If you have any technical issues, you discuss them with Sarah first.

**James:** Thanks for explaining.

## Vocabulary: Describing a Company’s Structure

When introducing a company’s structure, you can use these phrases:

To explain the general organization of a company: “Our company is structured into multiple departments.”

To describe who your direct manager is: “I report to [position].”

To explain a team’s role in the company: “This team is responsible for [task].”

### Example

Our company is structured into multiple departments. I work in the Software

Development Department, and I report to the Team Lead. This team is responsible for backend development and system optimization.
