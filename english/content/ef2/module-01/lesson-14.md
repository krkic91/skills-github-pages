## Role-Play

### Scenario: A manager talks to his staff about ways to increase workplace productivity in

his department. Study how the staff member persuades his manager with some clever ideas.

**Manager:** I called you to this meeting because I want your suggestions on how to increase productivity in your department.

**Supervisor:** Everyone can benefit from making a daily to-do-list. This is something employees can download from their mobile app.

**Manager:** That sounds useful. What else do you suggest?

### Resources

- [10-Year-Old Kid Shines in American Shark Tank Competition](https://youtu.be/BaUkuszb7dg?si=u4zX8xjhxihl7KPY)

<!-- Source: F2_M01_L14.pdf, page 2 -->

**Supervisor:** Sir, I think it is a good decision to take short breaks every hour. For me, it helps me stay focused at work.

**Manager:** I might have a few objections about that, but it is never too bad to try.

**Supervisor:** And finally, sir, I think you will agree with me on this – I believe phones should be put on silent during office hours.

**Manager:** I understand that it might be very hard, but again, it’s never bad to try. Thanks for your time.

**Supervisor:** I hope I presented some strong arguments. You are always welcome.

### Role-play Analysis

Answer the following questions:

1. What topic are they talking about?

2. What suggestions did the supervisor give his manager?

3. Which suggestions do you like and dislike?

4. Is the manager convinced about his staff’s ideas?

5. What are other ways you can think of in order to increase productivity at work?
