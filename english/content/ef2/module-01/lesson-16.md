## Role-Play

### Scenario: Alex and Jamie are talking about their phones. Study how their phone features

compete and what makes it better than the other.

**Alex:** Hey Jamie, check out my new Infinix phone! The battery life is insane.

**Jamie:** Really? That's awesome! My new phone's display is also amazing. I feel like I'm watching movies in 4K.

**Alex:** Cool! That’s really a gem. But I wonder how long it could last in a day.

**Jamie:** That’s the sad part. The display feature stands out, but the battery disappoints.

**Alex:** Oh no, with this new phone—I charged it once, and it lasted five days. That’s its main selling point.

**Jamie:** Wow, that’s impressive. My tablet can’t even last through a whole season of my favorite show without a recharge.

**Alex:** Haha, it’s like having a power bank built into my phone.

**Jamie:** That’s so cool. Let me finish what I’m watching now. Bye!

### Role-play Analysis

1. What gadget are Alex and Jamie talking about?

2. What features on their phones are doing great?

3. What makes Alex’s phone better than Jamie’s?

4. How long can Alex’s phone battery last?

5. Which features of a phone do you think are more important?

### Discussion

What phone features have evolved over time? What new changes do you think will happen in the future?

## Production

### MINI-PRESENTATION

Deliver a short, persuasive presentation (1–2 minutes) about your product, focusing on its features and benefits.

1. Use the outline from the previous activity (Explaining Great Features) to prepare your talking points. Focus on 3 key benefits or selling points.

### 2. Plan your presentation structure

- Introduction – Name your product and give a short background.

- Body – Explain 3 main benefits clearly and persuasively.

- Closing – End with a strong sentence that encourages interest or action (e.g., "That's why our product is a smart choice today").

### Example

Hello, my name is _________, and today I’m excited to present our latest product: the ____________. I will highlight the key features that set this item apart.

Use transitions words to organize

the sequence of your presentation.

Finally, _________________________________________.
