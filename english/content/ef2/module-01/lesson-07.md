## Role-Play

### Scenario: An employee, Sarah, is having trouble connecting to the internet. She calls IT

support, where Jake, an IT technician, helps her resolve the issue.

**Sarah:** Hi! This is Sarah from Marketing Department. I’m having a connectivity problem. I can’t access the internet.

**Jake:** Hi, Sarah. Let me walk you through the steps to check the issue. First, have you tried restarting your computer?

**Sarah:** Yes, I already did, but still not working.

**Jake:** Okay. This seems to be a configuration issue. Let’s check your Wi-Fi settings and see if you’re connected to the correct network?

**Sarah:** Oh, I see now. It was connected to the wrong network. Let me switch it… Yes, it’s working now!

**Jake:** Great! If the issue happens again, try restarting your router. If it continues, I’ll escalate this to the IT department for further troubleshooting.

**Sarah:** Got it. Thank you for your help.

**Jake:** No problem. Have a great day!

### Role-play Analysis

1. What problem is Sarah experiencing?

2. What is the first step that Jake asks Sarah to do?

3. What was the actual cause of the problem?

4. What phrase does Jake use to explain that the issue might be related to settings?

5. What does Jake offer to do if the problem happens again?

### Discussion

Have you ever faced a similar IT issue at work? How was it resolved?

## Production

### Instructions

- Pair-up

- One of you is IT support technician and the other is the employee with a technical issue.

- You will be given a different IT issue using the colorwheel.

- The IT technician must ask questions and help troubleshoot the issue.

- At the end, each pair presents their conversation in class.

### IT issues

1. Software Installation Problem

2. System Upgrade Required

3. Internet Connectivity Issue

4. Computer Not Responding

5. Escalating a Major Issue

### Discussion

1. What was the most challenging part of the conversation?

2. How can you improve your IT communication skills?

3. What new vocabulary did you use?
