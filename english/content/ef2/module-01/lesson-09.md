## Case study

The Delayed Launch (Voice Recording Transcript) "Catalyst Creative Solutions," an IT company based in the United States, was contracted by Kaito Electronics, a Japanese company, to develop a new cloud-based data management system. The initial project deadline was set for three months.

The U.S. team, accustomed to direct communication and frequent updates, sent daily emails detailing their progress and highlighting any potential roadblocks. They also scheduled weekly video conferences to discuss the project. Kaito Electronics, however, preferred less frequent communication and valued a more hierarchical decision-making process. They found the daily emails overwhelming and the direct questions during the video conferences somewhat abrupt. They also expected a more formal, detailed report closer to the deadline. As the deadline approached, Kaito Electronics expressed concern about the project's progress, citing a lack of detailed documentation and a perceived lack of understanding of their specific needs. The U.S. team, confused by the client's sudden dissatisfaction, pointed to the daily emails and weekly meetings as evidence of their consistent communication. Ultimately, the project launch was delayed by two weeks, leading to frustration on both sides. The U.S. team felt their efforts were unappreciated, while Kaito Electronics felt their communication preferences and cultural expectations were disregarded.

### Case study analysis

1. What contributed to the project's delay?

2. How did the differing expectations regarding project updates and documentation impact the client's perception of the US team's progress?

3. In what ways did the US team's approach conflict with the Japanese client's cultural preferences?

4. What steps could have been taken to better understand and adapt to cultural norms and communication preferences?

5. How could both teams have clarified expectations to prevent misunderstandings and ensure a smoother project timeline?

## Communication Essentials

### • Do Your Research

-Before interacting, learn about the client's or team's culture. -Focus on communication styles, formality, and expectations.

### • Communicate Clearly and Respectfully

-Avoid jargon and use simple language. -Be mindful of body language (even in video calls). -Ask clarifying questions to ensure mutual understanding.

### • Build Relationships

-Take time to establish trust. Show genuine interest in learning about their culture. -Be patient and understanding.

###  Adapt Your Approach

-Be flexible and willing to adjust your communication and work style. -Be open to feedback and learn from your mistakes.

<!-- Source: F2_M01_L09_v0.1.pdf, page 3 -->

### • Clarify Expectations

-Explicitly discuss deadlines, communication frequency, and project goals upfront. -Use phrases like "Let's clarify expectations” to avoid misunderstandings.

### • Understand Negotiation Styles

-Be aware that "Different cultures have different approaches to negotiations." -Be prepared to adapt your negotiation tactics.

### In essence

Listen actively. -Be aware of your own cultural biases.

-Show respect.

-Be flexible.
