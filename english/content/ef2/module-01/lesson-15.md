### DISCUSSION

4. How do you ensure a message is polite but clear when giving updates?

5. Have you ever worked with someone from a different culture? What did you learn?

6. What ’s one effective way to suggest a new idea in your team?

7. What do you focus on when trying to sell or present something persuasively?

8. How do you usually deal with a workplace problem or conflict?
