## Role-Play

### Scenario: Bryan (salesperson) attended an Expo and met a potential customer,

James.

### Role-Play Dialogue

**Bryan:** Hi, are you looking for a way to boost your team’s productivity?

**James:** Yes, we’ve been struggling to stay organized.

**Bryan:** Our product, TaskMaster, can help. This will help you by streamlining workflows and enhancing collaboration.

**James:** That sounds interesting. How does it work?

**Bryan:** It’s easy to use and integrates with your existing tools. Plus, this will help you increase productivity by 25% based on our customers’ feedback.

**James:** That’s impressive. What’s the cost?

**Bryan:** Compared to our competitors, we offer a very competitive pricing plan, and I’d be happy to provide a customized quote. Would you like to see the demo?

### Role-Play Analysis

Answer the following questions:

1. What is the customer’s pain point?

2. What solution did Bryan confidently propose?

3. What are the key benefits of the product that Bryan presented to the customer?

4. Did Bryan utilize social proof to convincingly persuade the customer? Explain.

5. What decisive action did Bryan take to encourage the customer to move forward?

## Vocabulary

1. This will help you by… – used to explain how a product or service will benefit the customer

### Example

- This will help you by reducing the costs and improving efficiency.

2. A key advantage is… – used to highlight a significant benefit or unique feature that sets the product apart from others

### Example

- A key advantage of our software is its user-friendly interface, making it easy for anyone to use.

3. Our product stands out because… – used to highlight the unique features, benefits, or value proposition that differentiate your product from others in the market

### Example

- Our product stands out because it offers real-time data analytics, enabling businesses to make informed decisions.

4. This will help you increase… – used to highlight the specific benefits and results that the customer can expect from using the product

### Example

- This will help you increase your sales revenue by up to 20%.

5. Compared to competitors, we… – used to identify unique selling points

### Example

- Compared to our competitors, we have a more competitive pricing strategy.

B. DISCUSSION: SAVING A STRUGGLING PRODUCT

Work in pairs or small groups on a brief case study about a struggling product.

### Your task is to

- Choose a product and analyze the situation.

###  Discuss and agree on how to reposition the product

o What value does this product still offer? o How can we differentiate it from competitors? o Who could be a new target market? o What is a compelling reason to buy it now? o How can we explain the ROI (return on investment) to business buyers or partners?

- Prepare 2–3 persuasive messages to pitch the revised product.

- Presents your new persuasive selling points. Keep it concise and focused.
