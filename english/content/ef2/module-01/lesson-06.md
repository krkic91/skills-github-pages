## Role-Play

Two IT professionals, Alex (the Project Manager) and Jamie (the IT Specialist), are discussing an ongoing IT project involving the development of a new cloud-based software application.

**Alex:** Hi Jamie, could you give me a quick update on our cloud-based software project?

**Jamie:** Sure! We're on schedule to meet our first milestone next week. The user interface is almost complete.

**Alex:** Great! Are there any important deliverables for this phase?

**Jamie:** Yes, we need to finalize the prototype and conduct initial encryption tests to ensure our data security.

**Alex:** Good thinking about cybersecurity. Have we considered feedback from our stakeholders?

**Jamie:** Yes, they want to ensure that our cloud-based application supports real-time collaboration securely.

**Alex:** What about the project scope? Do we need any adjustments?

**Jamie:** The scope is still on track, but we need to monitor any additions carefully to avoid scope creep.

**Alex:** Agreed. Let’s make sure everything aligns with our project goals and stays within the timeline.

**Jamie:** Absolutely! I’ll also prepare a risk assessment regarding third-party integrations.

**Alex:** Perfect! Thanks for the update, Jamie.

## Key Vocabulary

| TERMS | BRIEF DEFINITION | EXAMPLES |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud-based | Software or services hosted<br>on remote servers and<br>accessed via the internet. | Google Drive, Dropbox, and<br>Microsoft 365 are cloud-based<br>applications. |
| Cybersecurity | The practice of protecting<br>systems, networks, and data<br>from digital attacks. | Firewalls, antivirus software,<br>and data encryption measures<br>enhance cybersecurity. |
| Encryption | The process of converting<br>data into a coded format to<br>prevent unauthorized access. | Sensitive information like credit<br>card numbers is often<br>encrypted during online<br>transactions. |
| Prototype | A preliminary version of a<br>product used for testing and<br>feedback. | The team created a prototype<br>of the app to demonstrate its<br>features and gather user<br>feedback. |
| Deliverable | A specific outcome or tangible<br>result produced as part of a<br>project. | A completed software<br>application version is a<br>deliverable in a development<br>project. |
| Scope Creep | The uncontrolled expansion<br>of project requirements<br>beyond the original plan. | Adding new features to an<br>application after the project<br>timeline has been set can lead<br>to scope creep. |
| Integration | The process of combining<br>different systems or software<br>to work together seamlessly. | We completed the integration<br>of our new CRM system with<br>our email marketing platform to<br>streamline communications. |
